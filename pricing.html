Questions

Common questions.

How fast will my site be done?

Most Starter sites take about a week and Business sites about two, from the day I have your content. Timing depends mainly on how quickly you send photos and text.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, a few photos, and a short description of your services. If you are missing any of it, I will tell you exactly what to gather on the welcome page.

Do I own the website?

Yes. When the final payment clears, the site is yours. I hand over the files and help you connect your domain.

What does hosting cost?

I deploy your site on modern static hosting, and a typical small-business site usually runs on a free tier, so there is often no monthly hosting bill. Your domain name renews with your registrar, usually around $15 a year.

How does payment work?

Pay 50% to start and 50% when you approve the finished site, or pay in full upfront. Payments go through Stripe, so card details never touch my site.

What if I do not like the design?

You see a real draft of your homepage early, and revisions are included in every package. Full details are in the refund policy on the terms page.

Can you change things after launch?

Yes. Small edits are included on the Care plan, or I can quote larger changes as separate work.
